## Three candidate story structures
- **A. Your Business Never Stops:** doors close at night, an online presence remains. Rejected as too likely to imply customers and around-the-clock operations.
- **B. BEYOND YOUR FRONT DOOR (chosen):** one café becomes connected to a website, a purchased domain option, and business email, all as stations in one expanding diorama. Chosen for clear cause/effect and unique isometric pull-out rather than feature flash cards.
- **C. Customer's Journey:** one visitor follows website → message. Rejected as too little evidence of what EzSites helps the owner create.

**One subject:** Little Oak Café. **One goal:** establish an online presence. **One turn:** the route from the physical door branches into three digital touchpoints. **Bookend:** ‘OPEN FOR BUSINESS’ transforms into ‘GET YOUR BUSINESS ONLINE.’ **Signature shot:** one logarithmic pull-back reveals the connected system, never switches the isometric camera projection.

Tempo 120 BPM, 4/4 = 2s/bar, 15 bars, 30s, 24fps. Grid lines every quarter at 0.5s, eighth at .25s. Each 4s chapter = 2 bars except 24–26 =1 and 26–30 =2.
- Bars 1–2 (0–4): two notes on kalimba, bell on 0.5, sign on 1.0; diegetic café air.
- Bars 3–4 (4–8): blue route draws one segment per beat; add shaker/bass; first **near silence** at 7.5–7.75 before user action.
- Bars 5–6 (8–12): dry UI thock at 8.5, prompt text on eighth grid 9–10.5; preview sound **J-cut** 11.75.
- Bars 7–8 (12–16): web preview unfolds; publish click at 14.25; success stab at 15.0 and musical breath.
- Bars 9–10 (16–20): domain-tag stamp at 16.5; URL legibility hold; incoming envelope sound **J-cut** 19.75.
- Bars 11–12 (20–24): 20.5 envelope fold, 21.5 route motion, 23.25 settles; music decelerates.
- Bar 13 (24–26): scale reveal at 24.25 and **near silence** 25.25–25.75 before brand.
- Bars 14–15 (26–30): brand impact at 26; score resolves, 4-second logo/CTA hold; no last-second extraneous motion.
