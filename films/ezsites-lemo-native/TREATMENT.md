# EzSites: Get Your Business Online — Director's Treatment

Format: 15 seconds, 1080 × 1920, 24 FPS, English. One business, one connected online presence. No invented or unverified application interface.

## Three competing structures
1. **Feature list**: website → domain → email. Clear, but too much like a slideshow.
2. **Before/after**: local store → website. Simple but hides email and domain.
3. **One expanding business (chosen)**: a single physical storefront gains a digital home, domain identity and professional email. Camera and grid progressively reveal the entire connected system.

## Story / emotional arc
A small local business deserves to be visible. The store becomes a website; then domain and email snap into one connected workspace; EzSites signs off.

## Style benchmark
Swiss Motion Graphics: disciplined typography, moving grid, restrained signal blue, precise hard moves. Isometric Infographic: isolated isometric shop as consistent system metaphor. Learn camera pacing and layout. Do not copy upstream demo story, designs, music or scenes.

## Music, sound and mix
- 120 BPM, original electronic/percussive score, two build steps and a near-silent beat before final reveal.
- Foley: text clacks, swipe transients, module snapping, brand-end impact. Sound event timestamps come from `window.EV`, the same visual timeline.
- Duck music by approximately 10 dB during dialogue; final two-pass master −14 LUFS with true peak at or below −1.2 dBFS.
- Kokoro offline English voice. Word check with Whisper (ASR), never replace the voice with eSpeak in a successful production build.

## Text and brand QC
- Logo is the exact file from EzSites repository `apps/mobile-manager/assets/EzSites_full_logo_with_text.png` (source SHA `89038e52be405f1649771298c53aa49dad58dd0b`).
- Reject false app screenshots. The illustrated business site is a design metaphor and will remain visibly conceptual.
- Render stills at 1.8, 4.8, 8.6, 12.7, 14.3 seconds; review cut edges and all titles. Run Lemo's page readcheck and video blackdetect.
- Do not claim the user has multiple websites, BYOD or analytics: the ad only describes website, custom domain and business email.

## Acceptance
Actual MP4 H.264/AAC, 1080×1920, 24 FPS, 15 seconds, Kokoro narration verified by Whisper, no blank frames, official logo in ending, generated SRT, stills, credits and raw source.
