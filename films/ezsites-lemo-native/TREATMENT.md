# EZSITES / THE BUSINESS THAT FOUND ITS PLACE
Director's treatment — Lemo-Opuscar 20-second brand film · English · portrait 1080×1920 · 24 fps.

**Order of authority:** Upstream Lemo-Opuscar `AGENTS.md` → `DIRECTOR.md` → `TECHNIQUE.md` → `styles/swiss-motion/STYLE.md` → this product treatment. The project agent contract supplements but does not replace upstream direction.

## 1. Brief, verified facts, and creative premise
User's approved goal: real downloadable EzSites ad featuring **Get Your Business Online**, **website + custom domain + business email**, built with genuine app logo and native Lemo film renderer; professional sound and English voice. No unsupported features, no claims about conversion or performance, no screenshots falsely represented as the app. A conceptual sample business is explicitly represented as a design metaphor.

One subject / one goal / one turn: **a small storefront wants to be discoverable → its sign turns into an online presence → website, domain, and mail are all part of the same brand**. End on official EzSites mark.

## 2. Benchmarks selected *before* production, independent of Lemo demo
- **Swiss modernist kinetic typographic title design**: learn the precisely aligned grid, silence/re-entry timing, high contrast, disciplined type; do not copy any historical title sequence, proprietary brand, words, or shots.
- **Commercial UI typography / editorial product reveal**: learn confident hierarchy, legible mobile-first action and an expressive match cut from a physical sign into a digital address; do not borrow application UI, brand palette, original art, soundtrack or asset.
Chosen system: Swiss Motion Graphics. The small flat business drawing is composed as **printed geometric signage**, not an independent isometric engine/style. All solids have 2D material/print logic. Accent comes from the verified EzSites blue; official logo untouched.

## 3. Three candidate structures
**A. Features first:** announce WEBSITE / DOMAIN / EMAIL as three cards, then brand. Rejected: would repeat old infographic-list weakness.

**B. Search before/after:** shop vanishes among shops then a search query finds it. Rejected: might falsely imply live search/discoverability and adds nonverified search UI.

**C. ONE SIGN, THREE PLACES (chosen):** the physical shop sign pulls loose as a blue square; the square unfolds into a business website card, slides into an address/domain ribbon and finally folds into a message envelope. It lands in the official brand end card. Each chapter is *causally transformed* rather than a disconnected feature list. The single blue square is the remembered subject.

Opening: a 2D storefront with a single bright sign amid whitespace and the oversized hook **GOOD BUSINESSES / DESERVE TO / BE FOUND.** Ending echoes the blue square as an official brand mark with **GET YOUR BUSINESS ONLINE.**.
Signature native move: the blue sign **unfolds and reflows** into a real geometric business web page, while every neighbouring grid element rearranges in distance order. This is the emotional turn.

## 4. Logline / emotional arc
One little storefront with a bold blue sign makes the journey from local visibility to a connected online identity. Setup = small physical business; turn = sign becomes its digital home; payoff = same mark connects domain and email, and the official EzSites end card unlocks a clear next step.

## 6. Second-by-second beat sheet
0 signage signal; 0.5 hook; 1.5 push; 2.5 storefront hold; 3.5 **near silence** and square detaches.
4 page grid drawn ahead of the square; 5 square expands; 6 website hero; 7 short held view.
8 sound leads into domain reveal (**J-cut** at 7.8); 8.5 address ribbon draws; 9.5 domain resolves; 10.5 read hold; 11.5 **near silence**.
12 contact-line sound starts before section switch (**J-cut**); 12.5 envelope unfolds; 13.5 connection line reaches prior modules; 14.5 one breath; 15.5 anticipatory grid snap/near silence.
16 brand reveal full logo; 16.5 statement; 17–20 *hold CTA* without unnecessary movement, last notes of score resolve.

## 7. Musical cue map — WRITE BEFORE ANIMATION
**120 BPM**: quarter-note = 0.5 seconds, 4/4 bar = 2 seconds, **ten bars** total.
Sections on bar edges at 0/4/8/12/16 seconds. Every label, snap, line, module and camera landing aligns with quarter/eighth subdivisions (0.25 s).
- Bars 1–2 0–4: low motorik pluck + sparse closed hi-hat; hook at 0.5; deliberate downbeat gap around 3.5–3.75.
- Bars 3–4 4–8: add bass; square unfolds by 5.0; web page lands at 6.0; early domain J-cut at 7.75.
- Bars 5–6 8–12: precise electronic/woodblock clicks; URL types 8.75–9.75; bass ducked; 11.5–11.75 near silence.
- Bars 7–8 12–16: email folds and connecting lines at 12.5, 13.0, 14.0; harmonies widen; 15.5–15.75 near silence.
- Bars 9–10 16–20: one confident brand impact at 16; one final major harmony; CTA stays on screen ≥4 seconds.
Cut positions: 4.0,8.0,12.0,16.0; every SFX is emitted as an event from the same animation timeline.

## 8. Sound design / three layers
| Time | Ambience bed | Foley, material and J/L-cuts | Score state |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–4 | subtle abstract room-tone/air; not a specific shop location | sign lights: tiny glass tap; ruled line: dry pen; square detaches: paper slap | sparse pulse, first silence |
| 4–8 | quiet digital air | grid draw: paper slide; square → page: print clack; next address sound begins at 7.75 | bass enters |
| 8–12 | barely perceptible digital motor | URL: quick typewriter taps; blue ribbon snaps; silence at 11.5 | textured marimba/synth |
| 12–16 | low filtered room hum | envelope fold: paper snap; line-connect: click; brand whoosh begins before 16 | acceleration then silence |
| 16–20 | no new bed | brand: short paper stamp + tight sub; after-ring in tail | harmonic release & decay |
Music is ducked by ~10dB under voice, no hard gain edges. Lemo upstream two-pass master target −14 LUFS, peak ≤−1.2 dBTP. 2 near-silences and 2 transitions with early sound required.

## 9. Type, subtitle and titles
Primary type: system fallback Arial (no bundled proprietary font), one bold family. Strict left grid (x=58 on 720×1280 design plane), ink on cream, single EzSites-blue signal; maximum 2 headline lines per scene. Use clipped vertical reveals, precise ease, no fade. The title text must remain fully visible at least estimated characters/15+1.5 s or fail `readcheck.mjs`; 20s duration is chosen so each 4-second beat is legible.
Voice caption: bottom editorial label card, safe from mobile app overlays, stays ≥ max(1.8s, spoken+0.6s); separate UTF-8 SRT.
