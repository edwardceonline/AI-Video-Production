## Visual craft gate (must pass before full animation)
- **Storefront:** real-looking café, not cubes: varied brick tones and joints, window mullions/recess, dark glass with believable warm interior, fascia signage, shaded striped awning, potted plants, street furniture, scaled patrons, roof details, shadows and dirt/weathering.
- **Materials/lighting:** rough brick, dark painted wood, clear glass, woven canopy, metal lamps; directional sunlight with warm highlights, cool bounce and soft contact shadows; tone-mapped saturated photographic grade.
- **Lens/camera:** high 45–55° pitch for town frames; smooth camera separate from timelapse actor stepping; tracking tilt-shift sharp band, readable hero café and blurred near/far regions.
- **Resolution:** 9:16 1080×1920 final; 720×1280 proof okay but must verify subject size at 1080×1920.
- **Proof:** 3 source-derived stills at different times and a 3–5-second encoded motion clip, in GitHub Actions; no AI-generated image may be presented as film-rendered proof.
- **Comparison:** Generated cinematic storyboard from earlier chat remains *art reference only* and not a valid EzSites logo or UI screenshot.
