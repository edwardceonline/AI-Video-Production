# EzSites — Your Business. Online.

A 30-second **Remotion visual proof-of-concept** for a 9:16 ad about business presence, custom domains, professional email, and customer enquiries. This is an animation preview; licensed voice-over, music, and final mix await approval.

## Run

```sh
cd ezsites-commercial
npm install
npm run studio
npm run stills
npm run render
```

Main composition: `EzSitesBusinessCommercial` at 1080×1920, 30fps, 900 frames. Storyboard stills: `EzSitesScene01`–`EzSitesScene06`.

Assets: official EzSites full logo from `Games-Viet-GbR/Website-Assistant/apps/mobile-manager/assets/EzSites_full_logo_with_text.png`.

## English voice-over draft

| Time | Scene | Voice-over |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–4s | Business | You've built a business worth discovering. |
| 4–10s | Online presence | Give customers a professional place to find you online. |
| 10–15s | Domain | Make it yours with a custom domain. |
| 15–20s | Email | Stay connected with professional business email. |
| 20–25s | Enquiries | Turn interest into conversations with customer enquiries. |
| 25–30s | Brand | All from your phone. EzSites. Bring your business online. |

The fictional **New Day Studio** business, website content, people, and domain are illustrative artwork, not a real customer account or a promise of domain availability. No e-commerce, BYOD, analytics, or booking claims.

The same HTML generated by `src/visuals.js` powers both the browser preview storyboard and the actual Remotion compositions; `src/styles.css` carries the art direction. Scene-level entrance/exit animations are frame-driven.

The GitHub Actions workflow renders 6 stills and a silent half-resolution MP4 for review. Do not treat it as a final commercial until audio, licensing and quality checks are complete.
